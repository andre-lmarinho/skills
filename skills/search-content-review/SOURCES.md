## Keeping recommendations current

Recheck the relevant source when recommending a platform-specific tactic, citing a measurement feature, or encountering conflicting advice. Prefer current provider documentation for eligibility and controls. Describe experiments and observational studies with engine, population, method, and date; never turn a published percentage into a client's forecast.

Do not infer universal machine limitations from writing advice. A provider's recommendation for clearer prose does not prove that all models misparse a punctuation mark, require a FAQ, or discard context after a fixed number of words.

If browsing or factual evidence is unavailable, complete the editorial review, mark provider behavior or technical assertions as unverified, and request only the evidence needed for a material decision. When updating a rule, update its source, scope, and check date together.
