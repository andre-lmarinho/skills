## Maintenance

Update the affected rule and its checked date together. Follow links to confirm the original context before strengthening an advisory recommendation into a requirement. Keep a stable or versioned source when a document describes historical behavior.

The repository's rules about preserving contracts, limiting edits to scope, distinguishing evidence from intent, and verifying safely are engineering safeguards. They are not claims that the publishers require this exact workflow.
